## Why honkbox, not loop-lore

Only the server can register generated bytes in the artifact registry, allocate a `job_id`, and emit a `job_result`. loop-lore cannot synthesize an artifact id or make a job appear in the liveness-only `GET /v1/jobs` view from a base64 response.
